## Brand & Style

### Brand Personality & Philosophy
This design system embodies the heritage, grit, and culinary pride of Sonora: dry heat, crackling mesquite embers, cattle ranches, and prime cuts with world-class marbling. It combines an authentic northern Mexican butcher tradition (*el asador sonorense*) with the refined prestige of a luxury steakhouse boutique. The aesthetic feels deeply artisanal, authoritative, and unapologetically robust—steeped in smoke, iron brands, and prime cattle craftsmanship.

### Target Audience & Emotional Intent
Crafted for meat connoisseurs, grillmasters (*parrilleros*), culinary purists, and hospitality buyers who demand certified Sonoran quality (Hermosillo origin). The interface evokes tactile anticipation: the sizzle of high heat on cast iron, rich aged wood, butcher paper textures, and uncompromising premium standards.

### Design Movement
**Artisanal High-Contrast & Tactile Heritage:** A dark, moody aesthetic built on deep charcoal foundations, accented by ember-crimson flashes and desert-gold warmth. It employs heavy typography, sharp borders, and tactile textures that echo cattle-branding badges, butcher-cut diagrams, and dry-aged displays.

## Colors

The palette directly honors the brandmark, the fire of Sonoran mesquite, and the marbled muscle of high-grade beef:

- **Primary (`#C91D24` - Brasa Carmesí):** The vibrant crimson of cherry embers and prime raw beef cuts. Used for primary call-to-actions, price accents, highlight tags, and brand badges.
- **Secondary (`#C8963E` - Ocre Desértico / Dorado Tostado):** Sunbaked earth, toasted fat, and golden brass hardware. Applied to quality seals, marbling score ratings (BMS / USDA Prime), origin stamps (*Hermosillo, Sonora*), and secondary CTAs.
- **Tertiary (`#F4EFEA` - Marfil Hueso / Cuerno):** Warm bone-white and aged parchment. The primary reading color for typography on dark canvases, offering maximum contrast without the eye fatigue of sterile pure white.
- **Neutral Dark Tiers:**
  - Base Obsidian: `#0A0A0A` (Deepest canvas backing)
  - Mesquite Charcoal: `#111111` (Primary background)
  - Cast Iron: `#1A1A1A` (Card containers, drawers, and modal surfaces)
  - Dark Ash Slate: `#2B2B2B` (Borders, separators, and subtle divider rules)
  - Muted Bone: `#A39E98` (Secondary metadata, grams, and technical cut specs)

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy juxtaposes traditional butcher authority with sharp technical precision:

- **Headlines (Domine):** Chosen for its heavy, chiseled serif architecture, echoing vintage northern Mexico signage, iron brands, and heritage packaging. Headlines communicate provenance, lineage, and uncompromising craftsmanship. Uppercase variants are paired with subtle letter-spacing for category title headers.
- **Body (Hanken Grotesk):** A crisp, clean sans-serif balancing modern legibility with understated elegance. Delivers high clarity across cooking guides, cut origins, flavor notes, and shipping details.
- **Labels & Technical Data (Space Grotesk):** Applied to BMS marbling scales, butcher weights (oz/kg), aging duration (*35 Días Dry-Aged*), origin verification (*Hermosillo, SON*), and product SKUs. Always rendered uppercase or tabular for rigorous catalog clarity.

## Layout & Spacing

### Layout Structure & Grid
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column responsive fluid grid with 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters and generous 32px to 64px margins. Content is structured within deliberate editorial bands, giving full-bleed emphasis to cut marbling and texture imagery while retaining structural precision for data specs.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8-column layout with 20px gutters and 24px margins.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with 16px gutters and 16px lateral padding. Card layouts switch to focused vertical stacks or side-scrolling curated carousels.

### Spacing Rhythm
The rhythm relies strictly on a 4px/8px incremental base. Large hero sections and cut presentations receive breathing space (`space-xl` and above) to preserve a premium steakhouse gallery atmosphere, while technical cut selectors and weight-toggle pills maintain dense, efficient grouping using `space-xs` and `space-sm`.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not rely on light office shadows. In this dark, atmospheric theme, depth is conveyed through **tonal container layering**, **chiseled borders**, and **ember radiance**:

- **Layer 0 (Canvas Base):** Deep obsidian (`#0A0A0A`).
- **Layer 1 (Card & Drawer Surfaces):** Cast iron slate (`#1A1A1A`) enclosed with crisp 1px borders in dark ash (`#2B2B2B`).
- **Layer 2 (Elevated & Popover Elements):** Rich carbon (`#222222`) framed by warm ember or bone borders (`rgba(244, 239, 234, 0.12)`).
- **Ember Glow (Active States):** High-priority selections, interactive meat cuts, or primary buttons feature an ambient radial glow: `box-shadow: 0 4px 24px rgba(201, 29, 36, 0.35)`.
- **Vintage Trim:** Select hero cards and origin badges utilize double-ruled borders (1px solid inner, 2px outer gap) mimicking butcher certifications and aged packaging labels.

## Shapes

The shape system is firm, grounded, and masculine, favoring soft structural corners (`roundedness: 1`) over playful bubbles:

- **Base Radius (0.25rem / 4px):** Applied to buttons, inputs, badge tags, and weight selectors. It softens razor edges while maintaining an architectural, heavy-iron feel.
- **Card & Modal Radius (0.5rem / 8px):** For product tiles, butcher bundles, and checkout containers.
- **Origin Badges & Seals:** Circular and stamped silhouettes (full pill or 50% border radius) reserved exclusively for certified stamp graphics (*Calidad Sonora*, *100% Hermosillo*, *Inspección TIF*).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (Fuego Directo):** Background in Crimson (`#C91D24`), text in Bone (`#F4EFEA`), font `Space Grotesk` uppercase bold. Subtle ember glow on hover with a 2px lift effect.
- **Secondary (Hierro Forjado):** Background transparent, 1.5px border in Desert Gold (`#C8963E`), text in `#C8963E`. Hover fills with 12% golden wash.
- **Tertiary (Corte Butcher):** Deep charcoal surface (`#1A1A1A`), 1px subtle bone border (`#2B2B2B`), text in `#F4EFEA`.

### Product & Cut Cards (Ribeye, Tomahawk, Diezmillo)
- Built on `#1A1A1A` with a 1px `#2B2B2B` border.
- Features top badge for marbling grade (e.g., *Prime*, *Choice*, *High Choice Sonorense*) in gold or crimson pill styling.
- High-contrast macro photography of cut marbling with subtle vignette darkening towards card edges.
- Typography: Cut name in `Domine` bold, weight range and thickness (e.g., *1.5 pulgadas | 650g*) in `Space Grotesk`.

### Chips & Cut Selectors
- Compact pill-style toggles for weight, thickness (e.g., *3/4"*, *1"*, *1.5"*, *2"*), and cut condition (*Fresco*, *Añejado 28 Días*).
- Default: `#111111` with `#333333` border and muted bone text.
- Active: Border and text in `#C91D24` or `#C8963E` with charcoal surface fill.

### Input Fields & Selects
- Background `#111111`, border 1px `#2E2E2E`, text in `#F4EFEA`.
- Placeholder text in `#736F6A`.
- Focus state: Border transitions to `#C91D24` with a tight 2px outer ring glow `rgba(201, 29, 36, 0.25)`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- Heavy square corners with 2px radius.
- Unchecked: 1.5px border in `#444444`, dark center.
- Checked: Crimson `#C91D24` fill featuring a sharp bone-white checkmark icon.

### Meat Cut Profile Badges & Seals
- Specialized badge components designed to showcase the Hermosillo cattle provenance:
  - Stamp circular motif containing the bull's head silhouette.
  - Marbling indicator meter (1 to 5 flame/fat icons).
  - Recommended grill temperature indicator (Fuego Alto / Término Medio).
